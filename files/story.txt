ANTILL - A power-packed retro-roguelike about getting to the center of the Earth.

You play as INA (SÆRLIG-UDGRAVNING MK-IV): A highly specialized military digging robot that was born at the brink of human extinction.

PLOT LAYER 0
All of the remaining humans have been evacuated into a nearby space station. Your goal is to stop the sun from fully engulfing the earth. A new theory has emerged that states the Earth's core could be used to blast the sun away from Earth, which would give humans more than enough time to advance their interplanetary travel.

PLOT LAYER 1
The game takes place inside of a simulation. INA, the robot, is undergoing reinforcement AI training to perfect their military capabilities by putting her into an extremely, over the top stress test to accelerate learning. INA was designed and assembled by MODERKORP, also known as THE COMPANY.

PLOT LAYER 2
Lina was a robotics engineer who fell into a coma after a workplace accident. The "simulation" is Lina being in a coma. MODERKORP, a Danish phrase which translates to "MOTHER CORP", is a metaphor for your mother. Who accompanies Lina in the hospital room and has been the one giving you support.
