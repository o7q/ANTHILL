ANTHILL - A power-packed retro-roguelike about unearthing sinister horrors inside an alien planet.

You play as SUMI, short for SÆRLIG-UDGRAVNING MK.IV (SPECIAL-EXCAVATION MK.IV)
An advanced military mining robot equipped with a combat module.

You crash land on a remote alien planet, you have no recollection of who you are or where you came from. Leaving this planet is impossible, the surface temperature is near absolute zero. Your only option is down.
